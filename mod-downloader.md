ModioDirect is a standalone downloader for **mod.io** that allows users to fetch **public mods directly via the mod.io API**, without starting the official game client or launcher.

Some games (like Space Engineers) lock mod downloads behind ownership or a connected client. ModioDirect exists to solve that friction by providing a **manual, transparent way** to download mods you already have access to on mod.io.
