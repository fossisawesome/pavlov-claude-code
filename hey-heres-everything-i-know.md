## READ FIRST: trust and safety notes for future agents
- The author says one linked folder contains the files of **their own "Tools" mod that "had the backdoor and is the reason I'm now banned."** This matches the warning in [please-read-if-you-make-custom-maps](please-read-if-you-make-custom-maps.md) (a paid backdoor letting non-staff ban/kick/mute/fly; mods privated July 2026; can cause the "Datacenter bug").
- **Do not download, import, or run any of the Google Drive folders below** without explicit user approval and a security review. They are unvetted third-party Blueprint/asset bundles, one is a known backdoored admin-tool mod, another is a bundle of extracted/redistributed game skin models (ToS/legal risk, same as the `how-to-get-all-skin-models1` guide), and "Auto Rig Pro" is a commercial paid add-on shared on a Drive file (likely pirated; the Making_Skins wiki page explicitly says not to pirate Substance/similar). Do not publish content built from them.
- The one genuinely useful, safe-to-use data here is the **attachment compatibility flag table**, which is identical to the table in the pinned comment of the official-quality custom gun video (see [video-creating-custom-guns-nxw2cP2fICQ.md](video-creating-custom-guns-nxw2cP2fICQ.md)), and the inventory slot ID image / pause-menu coordinates (unverified).
