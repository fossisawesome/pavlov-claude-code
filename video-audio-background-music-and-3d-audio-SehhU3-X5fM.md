1. **Formats (UE 5.1):** only **`.wav`** is supported; later engine versions (5.7+) convert MP3/OGG automatically but **5.1.1 does not**. **Convert MP3/OGG to WAV first** (he recommends **Audacity**, to keep control of quality), then **Import** like any asset (purple sound-wave asset in your UGC).
2. **Background music/ambience (simplest):** drag the **WAV asset straight into the level**. It plays everywhere, but **stops after one pass unless Looping is ticked**: enable it per instance or by double-clicking the sound wave asset (**Looping** checkbox). Volume and pitch are adjustable there; you can assign a **Sound Class**.
3. **3D / spatialized audio:** create a **Sound Cue** (right-click in the UGC -> Sounds -> Sound Cue). The cue graph has an output; add a **Wave Player** node, assign the wave, tick **Looping** if wanted, connect to Output. Place the cue in the level: the sound pans between ears and gets louder as you approach. Two separate sources don't "cross stream" if each has its own cue/attenuation.
4. **Attenuation:** either **Override Attenuation** inside each cue/instance, or create a reusable **Sound Attenuation** asset (Sounds menu) and select it in each cue (better when many cues share a behavior). The attenuation shows an **inner sphere** and **outer sphere**: audible inside the outer volume, louder toward/inside the inner one (author is unsure of exact details). Other Sound menu assets: sound classes, attenuations, sources.
5. **Why a new map:** the earlier map wouldn't load on servers (suspected corrupted player info or game logic); migrating to a new map fixed it (a hint to keep GL/PlayerInfo clean).
