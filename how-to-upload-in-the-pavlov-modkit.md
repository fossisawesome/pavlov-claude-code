> Summary: How to Upload in the Pavlov Modkit 2026

---
- First, save all. 

Then, go to window, and then pavlov workshop. 

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/acb9/43026930/profile/screenshot2026-08-13180431.png)

Now that you're in the pavlov workshop, go to upload mod, select your mod, click android (for shack) and click upload.

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/acb9/43026930/profile/screenshot2026-08-13180757.png)

If you have any errors uploading, please message me on discord, pepsi.man15!
