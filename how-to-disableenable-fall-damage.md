# How to Disable/Enable Fall damage

> Summary: I will teach you how to disable fall damage!!!!

---
1. Go to your game logic

2. Right click, and search "Event Begin Play"

3. Drag off of Event Begin Play, and search "Set Fall Damage", the target should be Pavlov Game Logic.

This is what it should look like when your finished. 

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/8c8d/47191680/profile/screenshot2026-04-18213616.png)

Checking enable will let people take fall damage, have it not checked will disable fall damage.
