---
1 step

Download the 3D model

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27172727.png)

2 STEP

Scroll down to the bottom and find GLB and download it.

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27172843.png)

3 STEP

find the import button and click it

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27172933.png)

4 STEP

find your thing and open it at the bottom right of the file explorer. Then click import

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27173026.png)

5 STEP

Find the Asset Action button and from there click Create Blueprint Using This...

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27174258.png)

6 STEP

Scroll down to the plugins folder and find the UGC you want to put the blueprint into

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27174411.png)
\

7 STEP

If you cant put the blueprint in the folder that you want it in try renaming the asset.

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27174440.png)

8 STEP

Now you imported it as a blueprint GOOD JOB!! now look for the actor icon to find the right one.

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/ef2c/48791119/profile/screenshot2026-04-27174607.png)

 

NOW YOUR DONE THANK YOU VERY MUCH FOR FOLLOWING THIS GUID😁
