#### “I watched one/both of the tutorial videos above and now I know the basics. Where can I get answers for any additional questions I might have?”

 

- There are four areas of knowledge covering most of the Pavlov mod and map creation topics:

- Unreal Engine functionality

- Pavlov Modkit functionality

- Mixed functionality

- Blender & 3D software / 3D assets & models

 

 

- Unreal Engine functionality:

- Most of your content for Pavlov is basic Unreal Engine functionality and knowledge about this is widely available in rich quantities all over the internet.

- Unreal Engine 4 and Unreal Engine 5 shares a lot of features, so you’ll find that most guides for UE4 is also applicable to UE5.

- Some of the common questions you might have in this category:

- “Why are the shadows in my level too dark or bright?”

- “My map is laggy, how do I optimize it?”

- “How do i add a certain visual effect (fog, to my map or mod?”

- “How do i add zombies/AI to my map”

- “How do i syncronize actions and changes i created to other players? (Replication)”

- Pavlov functionality:

- Pavlov adds some functionality on top of Unreal Engine, which is represented in the Pavlov Modkit.

- The Pavlov Modkit makes it possible for content creators to tell Unreal Engine that they want to make content that integrates easily into Pavlov, such as vehicles, levels, skins and weapons.

- Some of the common questions you might have in this category:

- “How do I create a new game mode?”

- “How do I alter the damage taken by players?"

- “How do I add content from other mods to my level?”

- Mixed functionality (Unreal Engine& Pavlov Modkit):

- Functionality is not exclusive to either Unreal Engine or Pavlov.

- Blueprints used in Pavlov is often made up of primarily basic Unreal Engine nodes, but have some Pavlov specific nodes mixed in.

- Some of the common questions you might have in this category:

- “How do i make the player jump?”

- “How do i make a money system?”

- “How do i make an admin menu?”

- Blender & 3D software / 3D assets & models:

- 3D models and assets are likely going to be a significant element of whatever you decide to create, and a basic understanding of this topic will be a huge self-help.

- The Pavlov modding community will often refer to the free 3D software “[Blender](https://www.blender.org/)”, so, it is a great idea to get acquainted with its possibilities.

- Assets can be downloaded directly from the Unreal Engine marketplace. Follow [this link](https://www.fab.com/category/environment?ui_filter_price=1&is_free=1) for a collection of free models compatible with Pavlov (Unreal Engine 5.1)

- Some of the common questions you might have in this category:

- “How do i make my own 3D models?”

- “How do i add/remove certain features of a 3D model?”

- “How do i change where my model pivots?”

- “Where do i get models for use in my project?”

#### Tips and closing remarks

 

- Search for your question in relation to Unreal Engine first.
Chances are that you want to achieve something that is done in regular Unreal Engine, and not necessarily something specific to Pavlov

- Search for your question in relation to Pavlov second.

- Build your ideas in the basic unreal templates first, such as the Third person character template.
Once you have a functional prototype running in an Unreal Engine template, then you should have a fairly good understanding of what you want to achieve in Pavlov.

- The Pavlov creator community appreciates people who tried to solve the issue themselves before asking questions.
Explaining the things you have tried in order to solve your issue or achieve your goal to the community gives a good understanding of what you are trying to build and what direction you have taken with building it.

This makes it easier for everyone trying to help you – including yourself.
