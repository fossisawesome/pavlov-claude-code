### Making A Tele Porter Is Super Simple!

 

#### Join The Discord For More Help: [Join Here](https://discord.gg/ZbXJDt7ebW)

 

#### Step 1:
![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22010342.png)

Click One Of The Triggers. You Can Click TriggerBox, TriggerCapsule, TriggerSphere. But for the tutorial i will be using the TriggerBox.

 

#### Step 2:
![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22011105.png)

Open Your Event Graph

Delete every node except "**Event ActorBeginOverlap**"  

 

#### Step 3:
![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22011527.png)

Copy This

 

#### Step 4:
![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22011731.png)

Drag off from "**Dest Location**" and connect it to "**Get Actor Location**"

 

#### Step 5:
![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profile/screenshot2025-02-22012113.png)

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profile/screenshot2025-02-22012144.png)

#### How To Use The Teleporter?

 

Open Your Map

Then Go To Your Teleport BluePrint And Put It On Your Map Wherever You Want It

Like This

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22014242.png)

After That Drag In A Actor And Name It What Ever You Want

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22014535.png)

Click On The Teleporter And Look At The Side Of Your Screen Follow These Steps

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22014648.png)

Look For Your Variable Name

Then Click On It And Look For Your Actor You Make For The Teleport Location

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22014705.png)

Click On The Actor

![image](https://image.modcdn.io/members/32a4/30586497/profileteleport/screenshot2025-02-22014713.png)
